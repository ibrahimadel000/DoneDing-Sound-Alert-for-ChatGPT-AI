# Privacy Policy for DoneDing

**Last Updated:** November 2026

DoneDing ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices.

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
DoneDing does **not** collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, or AI chat content.

* **Local Storage:** The Extension uses local browser storage (`chrome.storage.local`) solely to save your preferences directly on your device, including:
  * Sound chime selections and volume levels
  * Notification toggles (desktop banners, top-bar alerts)
  * Enable/disable preferences for individual supported AI services
* **Chat Content:** The Extension monitors the generation state of responses locally within your browser tab to detect when generation stops. It does not inspect, read, log, or extract the contents of your prompts or conversations.

### 2. Permissions Used
* **storage:** Saves user configuration preferences locally on your machine.
* **notifications:** Displays an optional native desktop alert when an AI prompt finishes generating.
* **offscreen:** Hosts an offscreen audio document to synthesize and play completion alert sounds in the background.
* **tabs:** Determines whether the AI chat tab is focused or running in the background.
* **host_permissions:** Restricted to supported AI web chat services (e.g., chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com) to observe response generation state.

### 3. Third-Party Services
DoneDing does not use remote code execution, third-party trackers, analytics, or advertising networks.
